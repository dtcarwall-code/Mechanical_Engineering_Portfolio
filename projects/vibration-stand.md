# Additive-Manufactured Vibration Fixture

**Focus:** Mechanical test engineering · fixture design · additive manufacturing · vibration data analysis

## Project Overview

I investigated the viability of an additively manufactured vibration-test fixture as a faster, lower-cost alternative to a conventionally machined fixture. The work combined material screening, CAD, printing, controlled shaker testing, and response-data analysis.

## Engineering Approach

- Established a conventional fixture as the reference configuration.
- Held the mounting method and instrumentation approach consistent between comparisons.
- Developed and assembled an additive-manufactured fixture concept for controlled evaluation.
- Compared response behavior to identify damping, stiffness, and resonance tradeoffs.
- Documented the test method, observations, limitations, and next-step recommendations in a formal engineering report.
