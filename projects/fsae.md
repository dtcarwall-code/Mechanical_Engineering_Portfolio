# Formula SAE — Clutch & Shifter

**Team:** Cal Poly Pomona Formula SAE  
**Role:** Clutch & Shifter Captain · 2024–Present

## Project Overview

Formula SAE compresses a full engineering program into one yearly cycle: requirements, concept selection, CAD, analysis, manufacturing, integration, testing, and competition. I own the clutch and shifter subsystems and coordinate their interfaces with powertrain, electronics, controls, and the driver.

## Pneumatic Shifter

I developed sizing calculations for cylinder bore, stroke, working pressure, tank volume, and shift-cycle demand. Those calculations supported component selection and provided a repeatable way to check force and air-capacity margins before purchasing hardware.

## Electronic Clutch Development

The electronic clutch concept targets repeatable engaged, bite-point, and disengaged positions with adjustable actuation timing. My work includes actuator selection, force and motion calculations, mounting concepts, sensor strategy, and fail-safe considerations.

## Integration & Validation

The subsystem has to survive packaging limits, heat, vibration, and rapid service at competition. I use CAD reviews, manufacturing drawings, calculation checks, and physical fit-up to close the loop between the model and the car.

## What I Contributed

- Owned clutch and shifter CAD, component sizing, packaging, and design-for-manufacturing decisions.
- Built calculation tools to compare pneumatic configurations and maintain defined engineering margins.
- Coordinated interfaces with powertrain, electronics, and driver controls.
- Used Git-based collaboration, BOM/version control, and drawings to hand designs to fabrication.
- Led design reviews and helped newer team members develop CAD and subsystem-design skills.
